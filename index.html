ABOUT ME

Creative Technologist & UI/UX Architect

Hello, I am Jervi. I am a multidisciplinary designer and creative engineer dedicated to forging digital experiences that balance architectural elegance with precision engineering. With over eight years operating at the intersection of haute couture aesthetics and modern web technologies, I help pioneering brands articulate their vision through memorable, immersive digital narratives.

My background spans fine art direction, design systems architecture, and front-end engineering. I approach every project not merely as a set of screens, but as an editorial exhibition where typography, negative space, and micro-interactions coalesce to provoke emotion and inspire effortless user engagement.

"Great design transcends decoration; it is the physical and digital translation of intention, reverence for simplicity, and timeless human connection."
